Mastercard Offline Challenge

The client needed a short excerpt from her current programme to give event organisers an overview of her complex art, consisting of painting, improvisation and composition.


My goal was to visually enhance the mood of the music and the art through the contrast between clear, high-resolution and blurred shots with different lighting effects.


The piano and vocals were recorded with three different microphones.
