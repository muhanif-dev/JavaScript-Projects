Message From HOD

I would like to extend my warmest welcome to the Department of Computer Science & Information Technology. If you are interested in a computer science or engineering career, your journey starts here. The excellent infrastructure and best teaching faculty ensuring quality education such as interaction among students, parents and staff, along with a Training and Placement Cell ensure a bright future to its students. We strongly encourage innovation in research, in teaching, and in service to the profession, the local community, and industry. Our faculty and students are constantly striving to excel and to advance the state of the art in Computer Science. I invite you to be part of our efforts as we propel the Department of Computer Science & IT to ever-greater heights. In closing, I wish all the students and faculty a good academic career.
